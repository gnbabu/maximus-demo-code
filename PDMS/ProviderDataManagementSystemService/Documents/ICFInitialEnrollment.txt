<p>Effective [[CHOPEFFECTIVEDATE]], the Ohio Medicaid legacy number assigned to [[ENTERINGOPERATORNAME]] to operate [[FACILITYNAME]] is: [[EXITINGMEDICAIDID]]. Please keep in mind that the deadline for claims submission is one year from the date of service.</p>

<p>Federal law requires providers of health care and health plans to comply with the National Provider Identifier (NPI) regulations. ICF/IID claims submitted to ODM (Ohio Department of Medicaid) will be denied if the facility's NPI number is not in the required field(s) on the claim. Additional information regarding NPIs can be obtained by accessing the ODM website: http://medicaid.ohio.gov/</p>
