<b>Subject:</b> Fingerprint-Based Criminal Background Check Notice
</p>								

<p>The Affordable Care Act requires that all newly enrolling Medicaid providers and suppliers adhere to the new enrollment and safeguard screening requirements. In accordance with 42 CFR 455.434, high risk level providers must consent to fingerprint-based federal criminal background checks.  </p>

<p>The provider in the application as well as each individual with a five percent (5%) or greater direct or indirect ownership interest in a provider or supplier must provide fingerprints within thirty (30) calendar days of this notice. </p>

A complete list of BCI WebCheck vendors is available at the Ohio Attorney General website: <a href="https://www.ohioattorneygeneral.gov/">https://www.ohioattorneygeneral.gov/</a>.
