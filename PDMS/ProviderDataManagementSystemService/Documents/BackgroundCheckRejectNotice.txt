<b>Subject:</b> Rejection of Self-Supplied Background Check
</p>								

<p>This letter is to inform you that the Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM) <b><u>cannot</u></b> accept the enclosed FBI criminal background check(s) because the report(s) did not come directly from the Bureau of Criminal Identification and Investigation (BCI&I), London, Ohio. Effective with enactment of House Bill 119 on October 01, 2007, ODM can only accept criminal background check reports <b>if they are sent directly to ODM from BCI&I</b>. As stated in our initial notification of the fingerprint-based requirement letter, ODM can only accept criminal background check(s) <b>that are mailed directly to ODM <u>from</u> BCI&I. Additionally, only current reports will be accepted</b>.  </p>
<p>In order to comply with the fingerprint-based criminal background check requirement, you must obtain a new background check and have the results sent <u>directly</u> to ODM from BCI&I <b>or</b> write to BCI&I at the following address to request and pay for an updated “re-verification” of your recent background check: </p>
 <br /> <br />
<p class="center">Ohio Bureau of Criminal Identification and Investigation</p>
<p class="center">P.O. Box 365</p>
<p class="center">London, Ohio 43140</p>
<p class="center">877-224-0043 or 740-845-2000</p>
 <br /> <br />

<p>To request a (“re-verification”) of the fingerprints BCI&I has on file,<b>mail a request to the above address</b>. Include your name, address, telephone number, social security number, and a money order or certified check for $8 made payable to “Treasurer – State of Ohio”. Personal checks cannot be accepted. Request that the Ohio Bureau of Criminal Identification and Investigation send your criminal background check results directly to our address:</p>
<p class="center">The Ohio Department of Medicaid</p>
<p class="center">P.O. Box 183017</p>
<p class="center">Columbus, Ohio 43218-3017</p>
<p> <br />
<p>You may also take this document and request a new set of fingerprints. Be sure to ask the WebCheck vendor to mail the BCI&I background check results directly to the Ohio Department of Medicaid</p>
<p>The WebCheck vendor must type in ODM’s name and address in the “mail to” field on their computer screen and transmit to BCI&I. BCI&I will read the results of your fingerprints and BCI&I will then mail us a hard copy of the results.</p>
<p>If ODM does not receive your background check from BCI&I within 30 days of the date of your initial notification letter, your Medicaid application will be denied.</p>
