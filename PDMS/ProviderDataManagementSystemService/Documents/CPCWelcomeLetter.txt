<p>We look forward to working with your organization in achieving our shared goal for improving health outcomes for all Ohioans. In the coming weeks, you will receive additional information to support your participation in Ohio CPC. Feel free to contact us at (800)686-1516 or IHD@medicaid.ohio.gov if you have any questions.</p>
